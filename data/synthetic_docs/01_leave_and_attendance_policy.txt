1. PURPOSE AND SCOPE
This policy outlines the guidelines and procedures governing employee work hours, 
attendance tracking, paid time off, and statutory leave benefits across Acme Enterprise Solutions.
All full-time and contractual employees are covered by this policy.

2. STANDARD WORKING HOURS
2.1 The standard working week consists of 40 hours, from Monday through Friday.
2.2 Core working hours are between 10:00 AM and 4:00 PM local time. Employees are 
    expected to be accessible for collaborative meetings and department check-ins during core hours.
2.3 Flexible scheduling is permitted with the written approval of the direct team supervisor.

3. ANNUAL LEAVE ENTITLEMENT
3.1 Every full-time employee is credited with 24 days of paid annual leave per calendar year.
3.2 Annual leave accrues on a pro-rata basis at the rate of 2.0 business days per completed month.
3.3 A maximum of 10 unused annual leave days may be carried forward into the subsequent calendar year.
    Any accrued leave exceeding 10 days on December 31 will automatically lapse without financial encashment.
3.4 Requests for planned leaves extending beyond 3 consecutive business days must be submitted 
    at least 10 business days in advance through the internal HR Portal.

4. SICK AND MEDICAL LEAVE
4.1 Employees are eligible for up to 12 days of paid medical leave each calendar year.
4.2 Medical leave exceeding 2 consecutive working days mandates the submission of an authentic 
    medical certificate issued by a registered healthcare professional within 48 hours of resumption.
4.3 Emergency hospitalization or extended medical leave will be reviewed on a case-by-case basis by the Benefits Team.

5. PARENTAL LEAVE
5.1 Primary caregivers are entitled to 16 weeks of fully paid parental leave.
5.2 Secondary caregivers are entitled to 4 weeks of fully paid parental leave.
5.3 Parental leave can be taken anytime within the first 12 months following the birth or legal adoption of a child.

6. BEREAVEMENT LEAVE
6.1 Employees may avail up to 5 consecutive paid working days in the event of the demise of an immediate family member.
6.2 Up to 2 additional travel days may be granted if interstate or international travel is required.
